Tax disclaimer & acknowledgment

Reno Calc calculates estimates using tax rates entered by the user. Applicable taxes depend on location and the type of renovation work.

You are responsible for verifying applicable tax rates, tax treatment and all calculations before issuing an estimate. Reno Calc does not provide tax or legal advice and does not guarantee the accuracy of user-entered rates.

Your tax settings are saved on this device and reused in future estimates until you change them.
